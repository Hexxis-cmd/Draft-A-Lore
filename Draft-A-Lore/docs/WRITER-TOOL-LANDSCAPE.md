# Writer Tool Gap Analysis — Feature Landscape Reference

Research scope: [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview), [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/), [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/), [Campfire Writing](https://www.campfirewriting.com/), [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki), [Plottr](https://plottr.com/), [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/), [yWriter5](https://www.spacejock.com/yWriter5.html), [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) (+ [Longform](https://github.com/kevboh/longform/blob/main/README.md) and [World Builder](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/world-builder) plugins), [Twine](https://twinery.org/), [Ink/Inky](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/), [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/), and [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/). Supplementary sources: [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) and [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) for the export and revision categories, since they set the bar for those specific capabilities.

Legend: **Table stakes** = present in most mainstream tools in that segment; users now expect it by default. **Differentiator** = distinguishes one app from its peers, either because it's rare, unusually deep, or a defining reason people choose that app.

---

## 1. Drafting surface

| Feature | What it does for the writer | Apps that have it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Word-processor-style rich text editor** | Standard typing surface with bold, italics, underline, font/size choice, lists, images, and tables embedded directly in the manuscript text | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview), [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/) (Markdown-based), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/), [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/), [yWriter5](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) | Table stakes |
| **Distraction-free / full-screen composition mode** | Fades or hides toolbars, sidebars, and app chrome so only the current page/text is visible, often with a customizable backdrop image or color theme (Scrivener explicitly offers a "green-text-on-black" theme and a countryside backdrop) | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) ("Distraction-Free Composition," customizable full-screen themes), [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/) ("distraction-free environment"), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) ("Focus Mode" that blocks everything except the story, plus an Autofocus toggle), [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) ("Writing Sessions") | Table stakes |
| **Typewriter / autofocus scrolling** | Keeps the current line or paragraph vertically centered/highlighted while dimming surrounding text, so the eye doesn't have to track down the page | [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Autofocus enable/disable) | Differentiator (most competitors mention "focus mode" only at the whole-screen level, not line-level highlighting) |
| **Writing sprints / timed sessions** | Lets a writer set a timer and race against it (often with other writers) to build drafting momentum and speed | [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Weekly Writing Sprints, Story Craft Cafe community), [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Writing Sprints, timed community sessions to hit word-count targets) | Differentiator |
| **Word/character count goals — session, daily, project** | Lets a writer set a numeric target (whole manuscript, a single section, or a single sitting) and see live progress toward it | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (targets for manuscript, section, and session, live word/character counters, Writing History), [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/) (daily goals, deadlines, character-limit goals for blog posts), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Daily Word Count Goals, progress bars, live word-count feed, built-in NaNoWriMo tracker), [yWriter5](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) (Daily Word Count Target tool showing words-per-day needed and percent complete), [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/story-planner) (word-count goals and writing statistics in the Manuscript Module) | Table stakes |
| **Find & replace** | Searches within a single document or across the whole project/manuscript and swaps text | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (project-wide search plus in-document Find bar with replace), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (search entire novel, replace everywhere at once) | Table stakes |
| **Multi-document / split view editing** | Shows two or more documents (e.g., an earlier chapter and the current scene, or research and prose) side by side in the same window | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Split Editor, up to four documents; Quick Reference panel on iPad), [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Multi Window Support with draggable additional view panes) | Differentiator |
| **Scrivenings / merged multi-document view** | Lets the writer treat several separate scene/chapter documents as one continuous scrollable text for editing, while keeping them as separate files in the outline | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Scrivenings mode) | Differentiator (unique naming/mechanic to Scrivener; other tools achieve similar results only via compile/export) |
| **Footnotes and comments/annotations inline** | Lets the writer attach editorial notes, citations, or continuity flags to specific text without it appearing in the final draft | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (footnotes for academic writing, inline comments and annotations, highlighted "needs work" phrases), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Comments attached directly to manuscript text, Sticky Notes placed anywhere, color highlighting to flag revisions) | Table stakes for long-form/academic tools; still a **differentiator** among lightweight fiction-only apps that lack it |
| **Live spelling/grammar/style checking as you type** | Underlines or flags issues for capitalization, punctuation, redundancy, and style directly in the editor | [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/) (built-in grammar/style check with suggestions across 20+ languages), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (spell check, "Advanced Checks" for grammar/spelling/style, "Read to Me" text-to-speech proofing), [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (grammar/style feedback as-you-type, available inside Scrivener and MS Word via Desktop Everywhere integration) | Table stakes |
| **Markup/Markdown-based writing with live preview and on-the-fly style switching** | Lets a writer type in a lightweight plain-text markup and see (or export to) fully styled output without leaving the keyboard | [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/) (markup editor, live preview, "on-the-fly switching of styles"), [Twine](https://twinery.org/) (formats add CSS/JavaScript styling on top of passage text) | Differentiator |
| **Custom text styles / formatting presets applied with one click** | Predefined paragraph/character styles (e.g., block quote, heading) that can each render differently depending on the export target (ebook vs. PDF) | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Styles/Formatting Presets), [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) (Book Styles applied to every chapter, including ones added later) | Differentiator |
| **Highlighting / color-coded text flags** | Marks passages needing attention (revision, tone check, plot flag) with visual color coding directly in the draft | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Text Highlighting for revisions/tracking) | Table stakes |
| **Cross-platform sync with offline access** | Keeps the same manuscript editable and current across desktop, tablet, and phone, with edits merging automatically | [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/) (library sync across Mac/iPad/iPhone), [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Dropbox-based sync, iOS/macOS/Windows), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (multi-device access, autosave cloud sync, works offline), [Atticus](https://www.atticus.io/) (Progressive Web App, works online/offline on any device with a browser) | Table stakes |

## 2. Structure and planning

| Feature | What it does for the writer | Apps that have it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Binder / project tree sidebar** | A single navigable sidebar listing every chapter, scene, character sheet, and research note in the project, reorderable by drag-and-drop | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (the Binder), [Ulysses](https://ulysses.app/) (folders for scenes/chapters), [yWriter5](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) (chapter/scene list) | Table stakes |
| **Corkboard / index-card view** | Represents each scene or chapter as a virtual index card carrying a short synopsis, so the writer can plan and reorder structure without seeing full prose | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Corkboard, freeform card placement, colored storyline lines), [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/story-planner) (Index Card View / corkboard-style canvas in the Manuscript Module, zoom out for bird's-eye view) | Table stakes for full novel-writing suites; **differentiator** for planning-only tools that lack a manuscript editor |
| **Outliner / spreadsheet-style overview** | Tabular view of the whole manuscript or a chapter, listing word count, status, labels, and custom metadata per section, sortable and with configurable columns | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Outliner View), [yWriter5](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) (Scene List columns: viewpoint, words, status, action/reaction, characters, locations, items) | Table stakes |
| **Story-structure / beat-sheet templates** | Ready-made frameworks (three-act, Hero's Journey, Save the Cat, Fichtean Curve, etc.) that a writer can drop scenes into instead of designing structure from scratch | [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) (40+ templates across genres including LitRPG), [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/story-planner) (10+ named templates: 3 Act Structure, 7 Point Structure, 27 Chapter Method, Fichtean Curve, Heroine's Journey, Hero's Journey, Romancing the Beat, STC Story Beat Sheet, Story Circle, Story Grid), [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Story Canvas guided prompts, Plot Analysis against missing story beats) | Table stakes among planning-focused tools; **differentiator** for pure drafting apps (Ulysses, yWriter, Dabble) that don't offer them |
| **Visual/drag-and-drop timeline** | A horizontal or Gantt-style board where scenes, chapters, and character arcs can be dragged into chronological order, often supporting multiple parallel timelines (e.g., one row per POV character) | [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) (visual timeline, drag-and-drop scenes/chapters/character arcs), [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/timeline-maker) (nested-note timeline, Gantt chart view, flowchart-style event connections, custom fictional calendar linked bidirectionally to the timeline), [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) (session/campaign timeline tracking, though not marketed as a dedicated visual timeline board on this page) | Differentiator |
| **Series bible / cross-book continuity store** | A single project or shared database that holds characters, places, and notes for every book in a series so continuity details don't have to be re-entered per book | [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) (series bible, one project can hold every book in a series, tested up to 47-book series), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Series & Shared Worlds sharing characters/locations/lore across books), [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/) (shared Codex across books in a series) | Differentiator |
| **Character sheet templates and tracking** | Structured forms for character bios, goals/motivations, and appearance, often with named planning frameworks (Goal-Motivation-Conflict, Snowflake Method, Enneagram) | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (character-sheet templates, custom icons), [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) (character sheets with templates for GMC, Snowflake, Enneagram, Rorschach), [yWriter5](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) (Bio/Notes/Goals/Picture tabs, major vs. minor character flag), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Character Profiles) | Table stakes |
| **Scene metadata (POV, status, labels, importance, scene type)** | Tags each scene with structured attributes — point-of-view character, draft status (outline/draft/1st edit/etc.), color label, main-plot-vs-subplot, action-vs-reaction — that can be filtered, sorted, or reported on | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (custom metadata, status, labels, Collections/smart lists e.g. a "Flashbacks" collection), [yWriter5](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) (Viewpoint, Status, Action/Reaction type, Importance: main plot/subplot, per-scene Goal/Conflict/Outcome fields), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Plot Labels tagging theme/POV/tension) | Table stakes for structure-focused tools |
| **Drag-and-drop reordering of scenes/chapters** | Rearranges structure by dragging cards, outline rows, or list items instead of cutting and pasting text | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview), [Plottr](https://plottr.com/), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Drag-and-Drop Organization for chapters/scenes), [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/story-planner) (drag-and-drop chapter rearrangement), [yWriter5](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) | Table stakes |
| **Freeform mind-mapping / canvas for brainstorming** | An open canvas for placing and connecting idea nodes, useful for plotting relationships, plot holes, or worldbuilding before formal outlining | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) via companion app [Scapple](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (freeform mind-mapping, brainstorming plot/character/setting ideas, mapping plot holes), [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/timeline-maker) (canvas for freely arranged, connected cards before structuring a timeline), [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) (Canvas: "infinite space for research, brainstorming, diagramming") | Differentiator |
| **Non-writing "pantser" support mode** | Tools designed to be used *after* a discovery-written first draft to retroactively map plot, find plot holes, and reconcile continuity, rather than only for upfront outlining | [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) (explicitly supports "pantsers," "plantsers," and other non-outliner writer types working after drafting), [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/) ("planning modes" for pinpointing plot holes and inconsistencies at any stage) | Differentiator |
| **AI-assisted plot-hole / structural analysis** | Uses an LLM to scan an outline or draft and flag pacing problems, missing story beats, or structural weaknesses | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Plot Analysis scanning for pacing issues and missing beats; Manuscript Analysis giving an "editor-like overview" of plot/pacing/setting), [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/help/getting-started/getting-started-with-ai/why-should-i-use-ai) (custom AI prompts discussing plot holes, subplots, narrative flow, opening hooks) | Differentiator |
| **Project/series-wide word-count and progress statistics** | Rolls up word counts, deadlines, and status across chapters into dashboards or reports | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Writing History, per-section word counts in Outliner), [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/story-planner) (writing statistics and deadline/accountability tracking in Manuscript Module), [yWriter5](https://www.pcmag.com/reviews/ywriter) (chapter word/scene counts, character-appearance-count reports) | Table stakes |

## 3. Revision and review

| Feature | What it does for the writer | Apps that have it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Snapshots / saved document versions** | Freezes a copy of a document (or the whole project) before a big rewrite so the writer can compare or revert without a manual backup file | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Snapshot per section, roll back with one click, "Compare" feature to show what changed) | Differentiator (few competitors offer per-section snapshotting outside Scrivener) |
| **Version diff / compare** | Shows exactly what text changed between two saved versions of a document | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Compare feature between snapshots) | Differentiator |
| **Multiple parallel drafts of the same project** | Lets a writer maintain more than one version of a manuscript (e.g., an editor's cut vs. author's cut) as clearly separated but related projects | [Longform for Obsidian](https://github.com/kevboh/longform/blob/main/README.md) (drafts implemented as same-titled projects grouped and renamable in the Project tab) | Differentiator |
| **Revision mode with color-coded edit tracking** | Marks new text added during a specific editing pass in a distinct color so the writer can see what changed in that pass at a glance | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Revision Mode) | Differentiator |
| **Inline comments and threaded feedback** | Lets an author, editor, or beta reader attach a note to a specific passage without altering the manuscript text itself | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Comments and Annotations), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Comments directly on manuscript text to flag edits/questions/ideas) | Table stakes for collaborative workflows |
| **Sticky notes / free-floating annotations** | Notes that can be pinned anywhere in the manuscript to capture a stray idea, question, or reminder without disrupting the flow of the draft | [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Sticky Notes placed anywhere in the manuscript) | Differentiator |
| **Text highlighting for revision tracking** | Color-highlights passages to flag them for a specific kind of pass (e.g., red for continuity issues, yellow for line edits) | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) | Table stakes |
| **AI-driven continuity / consistency checking** | Uses custom prompts or a chat interface to scan a scene, chapter, or full manuscript against the outline or Codex/wiki and flag plot inconsistencies, historical inaccuracies, or worldbuilding contradictions | [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/help/getting-started/getting-started-with-ai/why-should-i-use-ai) (explicit "Editorial Feedback" custom prompts for plot inconsistencies, historical inaccuracies, worldbuilding contradictions, using outline/scene/full-novel context) | Differentiator — currently rare outside AI-native tools |
| **Automated consistency checks (spelling, capitalization, hyphenation, names)** | Flags inconsistent spelling variants, capitalization, or hyphenation of the same term across a long manuscript | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features/writing-reports) (Consistency checks for spelling/capitalization/hyphenation, standardizes usage) | Differentiator |
| **Readability analysis** | Scores how easy a passage is to read for its intended audience (though most vendor pages describe this only qualitatively rather than naming a specific formula like Flesch-Kincaid) | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Readability analysis/scores, though the page does not name a specific formula) | Table stakes among grammar/style tools; **differentiator** for novel-writing suites, most of which lack any readability metric |
| **Dialogue analysis (tags, pacing, patterns)** | Reviews dialogue-heavy passages for repetitive tags, pacing issues, or unnatural conversational rhythm | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Dialogue analysis, Dialogue-tag analysis, Dialogue-pacing analysis, Character-conversation analysis) | Differentiator |
| **Pacing / structure / sensory-balance reports** | Flags sections of a manuscript that move too slowly or too quickly, checks whether a story hits expected structural beats, and checks how sensory detail (sight/sound/touch/etc.) is distributed | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features/writing-reports) (Pacing Report, Structure Report, Sensory Report — all noted as more accurate on unlimited/long-document runs) | Differentiator |
| **Chapter Critique / Manuscript Analysis (AI editorial pass)** | Gives instant feedback on a single chapter's strengths/weaknesses, or an "editor-like" overview of plot, pacing, and setting across a full manuscript | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Chapter Critique, Manuscript Analysis) | Differentiator |
| **Virtual beta reader simulation** | Simulates how a reader experiences the story — hook points, emotional impact, and drop-off points — without needing a human beta reader | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Virtual Beta Reader) | Differentiator — novel category, not yet common |
| **Overused word/phrase, repetition, and cliché detection** | Flags words, phrases, or clichés used too often across a document, with the ability to ignore quoted dialogue | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features/writing-reports) (Overused Word Report, Overused Phrase Report, Repeats Report, Cliché Report, Vague Language Report, Pronoun Report, Alliteration Report) | Differentiator (deep coverage); basic repetition flags are becoming table stakes in general grammar tools |
| **Plagiarism checking** | Verifies text against external sources to catch unintentional reuse of others' words/ideas | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features/writing-reports) (Plagiarism Checks, with separate usage limits from other reports) | Differentiator |
| **Marketability / query-letter analysis** | Analyzes a manuscript to identify target audience and generate agent-ready pitch materials | [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Marketability Analysis, Querying Workshops) | Differentiator |
| **Grammar/style/technical scripting checks for interactive fiction** | Flags scripting errors (not prose errors) — invalid property values, invalid references, duplicate technical names, defective assets | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Conflict Search: detects invalid property values, invalid references, duplicate technical names, defective assets), [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/) (indentation-mismatch errors surfaced automatically) | Differentiator, and category-specific (see Section 6) |

## 4. World-building and reference

| Feature | What it does for the writer | Apps that have it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wiki/codex entries for characters, places, lore, items** | A structured, searchable database of story-world entries that exists independent of the manuscript, editable while writing | [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) (articles for lore, NPCs, locations, monsters, adventures, characters, items), [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/) (the "Codex" wiki for characters/places/lore), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Worldbuilding Bible for lore/maps/history/rules), [Campfire](https://www.campfirewriting.com/) (18 dedicated worldbuilding modules: Characters, Species, Maps & Locations, etc.), [Obsidian World Builder plugin](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/world-builder) (structured entries for characters, locations, factions, lore, timeline events) | Table stakes for worldbuilding-oriented tools; **differentiator** for pure drafting apps that lack any dedicated wiki layer (Ulysses, yWriter) |
| **Automatic entity linking / mentions system** | Detects or lets a writer quickly link a name in prose to its full codex/wiki entry, in under a few seconds, without breaking writing flow | [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) ("link one article to another in less than 3 seconds," quick pop-up button to create/link articles while writing another), [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/) (automatic tracking and linking of Codex entries) | Differentiator |
| **In-line reference lookup while writing** | Lets a writer search or edit the world/wiki without leaving the manuscript editor, to check or add a detail mid-sentence | [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) ("search and edit your world from the writing interface... without losing writing flow"), [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/) (Codex insight surfaced during writing) | Differentiator |
| **Relationship maps / entity graphs** | Visualizes connections between characters, factions, or locations as a node graph rather than a flat list | [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md/) core Graph view (visualizes relationships between notes, "helps users find hidden patterns"), [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) via [Scapple](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (visualizing relationships and interactions between characters) | Differentiator — notably **absent** from World Anvil, Plottr, and Campfire's own marketing copy despite those tools' worldbuilding focus |
| **Shared world/Codex across a series** | Lets a writer reuse the same character, place, and lore entries across every book in a series without copy-pasting | [Novelcrafter](https://novelcrafter.com/) (share a Codex across books in a series), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Series & Shared Worlds), [Plottr](https://plottr.com/) (series bible holding characters/places/notes for an entire multi-book project) | Table stakes among series-oriented planning tools |
| **Structured entity templates with custom fields** | Lets a writer define reusable schemas for entity types (e.g., every "Character" gets Name/Role/Age/Faction/Goals/Secrets fields) rather than free-text notes | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (template-based object properties; entities set up with editable, modular templates that objects inherit from), [Obsidian World Builder plugin](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/world-builder) (structured fields per entry type: Characters get Name/Role/Age/Faction/Physical description/Personality/Goals/Secrets; Locations get Type/Parent location/Inhabitants; Factions get Alignment/Enemies/Allies), [yWriter5](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) (fixed Bio/Notes/Goals/Picture tabs per character) | Differentiator |
| **NPC/stat-block generation for game masters** | Quickly generates non-player characters or pulls up homebrewed stat blocks tied to a specific game system, for use during a live tabletop session | [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) ("Create NPCs in seconds," "pull up homebrewed stat blocks from a world or game system," Digital DM's Screen) | Differentiator — unique to RPG/campaign-management-flavored worldbuilding tools |
| **Full-text search across the entire wiki/world** | Searches every article/entry in seconds, often combined with tags and categories for filtering | [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) (quick search parses the entire wiki "in seconds"), [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Project Search finds any line across hundreds of documents), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (search entire novel for any word/phrase/name) | Table stakes |
| **Tagging and categorization of entries** | Organizes wiki entries into categories and free-form tags to make large worlds navigable | [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) (categories plus tags), [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Plot Labels for tagging scenes/chapters), [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (keywords, custom metadata, Collections) | Table stakes |
| **Contextual thesaurus / dictionary lookup** | Suggests synonyms or definitions in-context without leaving the writing surface | [Dabble](https://www.dabblewriter.com/) (Contextual Thesaurus), [ProWritingAid](https://prowritingaid.com/features) (Replacement word suggestions, contextually relevant) | Table stakes |
| **Privacy tiers / partial public sharing of world content** | Lets an author keep a wiki private, share select articles publicly (e.g., a series bible for readers), or grant scoped access to specific collaborators like beta readers or players | [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) (private wikis, public sharing, "share selected parts of a game wiki with players," managing beta-reader waves) | Differentiator |
| **Visual map-building tools for locations** | A drawing/layer-based canvas for sketching in-world maps, with the ability to add pins, text, and interactive hotspots | [Campfire](https://www.campfirewriting.com/) (Interactive Maps module), [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Location Editor: vector-based 2D drawing tool with layer management, background images, drawing forms and paths) | Differentiator |
| **Conlang / constructed-language tools** | Dedicated support for building fictional languages as part of a world | [Campfire](https://www.campfirewriting.com/) (Conlang Software module) | Differentiator — rare, niche worldbuilding capability |
| **Custom fictional calendars linked to timeline** | Lets a writer define non-Earth calendar systems (custom eras, months, moons, seasons) that stay synchronized bidirectionally with the story's plotted timeline | [Campfire](https://campfirewriting.com/timeline-maker) (custom calendar linked both ways to the timeline) | Differentiator |

## 5. Export and publishing

| Feature | What it does for the writer | Apps that have it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Compile / build system with reusable presets** | Converts a structured project (many documents/scenes) into a single output document using a saved configuration, rather than manually assembling text | [Scrivener](https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview) (Compile: "one project, every output"), [Longform for Obsidian](https://github.com/kevboh/longform/blob/main/README.md) (Compile tab lets users build custom multi-step workflows to turn a project into a manuscript), [Vellum](https://vellum.pub/) (rebuild ebook and print editions "in seconds" after edits, keeping every edition in sync) | Table stakes among full manuscript-preparation tools |

## 6. Interactive fiction / RPG authoring

| Feature | What it does for the writer | Apps that have it | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visual node-graph / flow editor for branching narrative** | A drag-and-drop canvas where each story beat, dialogue line, or choice is a node connected by lines representing player paths, letting the author see branch structure at a glance | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Flow Editor: visual drag-and-drop composition of branching stories, nested flows where objects can contain other objects, e.g., dialogues nested inside scene objects), [Twine](https://twinery.org/) (passage map is the core editing metaphor, though the vendor page itself doesn't describe it in detail) | Table stakes for dedicated IF/dialogue tools (Articy, Twine); **absent** from text-only scripting tools like ChoiceScript and Ink's raw source view (Ink's node view exists only via its Inky editor's play pane, not as a graph) |
| **Variables and stats tracking** | Lets the author define named values (numeric, boolean, or string) that persist across the story and change based on player choices, driving later branching or a visible "stats screen" | [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/) (`*create`/`*set` variables — numeric with +/-/*// operators, string, and boolean types; results shown on a player-facing "Stats Screen"), [Twine](https://twinery.org/) ("stories can be extended with variables"), [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (global variables of Boolean/Integer/String type, plus object properties addressable in scripts, e.g., gating a dialogue choice on whether the player learned a skill) | Table stakes for all scripting-based IF tools |
| **Conditional logic / branching on state** | Lets specific text, choices, or scenes appear only if a condition on a variable or property is met (`*if`/`*elseif`/`*else` in ChoiceScript; Conditions/Instructions in Articy) | [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/) (`*if`, `*elseif`, `*else`, numeric comparisons), [Twine](https://twinery.org/) ("stories can be extended with... conditional logic"), [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Conditions determine which choices appear; Instructions modify variables/properties based on the chosen path; syntax highlighting and auto-completion for the scripting language) | Table stakes |
| **Choice-based branching with nesting and reusable jumps** | Presents the reader/player with options; each option can lead to a nested sub-choice, jump to a named label within the same file (`*goto`), or jump to an entirely different scene file (`*goto_scene`), enabling reusable branch structures across the whole game | [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/) (`*choice`, `*goto`/`*label` for in-scene jumps, `*goto_scene` for cross-file jumps, `*scene_list` defining scene sequence, `*ending` auto-inserting a "Play Again" menu), [Ink](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/) ("highly branching narrative," diverts with jump-to-definition support in Inky), [Twine](https://twinery.org/) (nonlinear story structure) | Table stakes |
| **Inventory/item and world-state tracking** | Represents discrete objects a player can pick up, use, or lose, tied into the branching logic (this is closer to game-design territory than pure IF, and is comparatively under-marketed even by the RPG-focused tools) | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Entities can represent "Items," among Characters/Enemies/Weather/Time, tracked in the Game Object Database), [World Anvil](https://www.worldanvil.com/features/worldbuilding-wiki) (item articles as wiki entries, not live game state) | Differentiator — no reviewed tool exposes a first-class, ready-made "inventory system" out of the box; Articy comes closest via generic typed Entities, but the writer must build the logic |
| **Live playtesting inside the authoring tool** | Lets the author play through the branching story as a reader would, immediately, without leaving the editor, seeing exactly which choices lead where | [Ink/Inky](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/) (play pane that auto-refreshes and reloads choices made as the author edits; Unity plugin includes a simple in-editor previewer to "play stories directly within the Unity Editor without writing a line of code"), [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Simulation mode: presents the story flow "in a PowerPoint-like presentation" including live conditions/instructions, so the chain of events can be tested before handing content to a game engine), [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/) (a local server must be launched so edited scene files can be reloaded and played in-browser) | Table stakes for dedicated IF/narrative-design tools; the mechanism ranges from a full in-app simulator (Articy) to a lightweight local server refresh (ChoiceScript) |
| **Error highlighting / as-you-type validation** | Flags scripting errors immediately while writing rather than only at compile or playtest time | [Ink/Inky](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/) ("error highlighting as you type," Alt-click jump-to-definition on a divert), [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/) (displays an explicit error message when indentation levels within nested choices don't line up) | Table stakes |
| **Project-wide technical linting / conflict search** | Scans the entire project database (not just the current file) for structural problems: invalid property values, dangling/invalid references, duplicate technical names, and defective/missing assets | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Conflict Search, described explicitly as a "technical search" distinct from Simulation mode's narrative playtesting) | Differentiator — no other reviewed IF tool offers a dedicated project-wide integrity scan separate from runtime playtesting |
| **Live property/variable inspector during simulation** | Shows the current value of every tracked variable/property in a side panel while stepping through a playtest, so the author can confirm state changes are firing correctly | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Property Inspector: "live view of property changes while going through the game's story" during simulation) | Differentiator |
| **Game-engine export/integration (Unity, Unreal)** | Provides a dedicated importer or runtime plugin so the branching content authored in the tool can be dropped directly into a shipping game, including automatic dialogue/flow traversal at runtime | [Ink](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/) (Unity plugin with fully customizable flow traversal engine and automatic recompilation of edited ink files; Unreal integration via the open-source Inkpot plugin from The Chinese Room), [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (dedicated Unity importer with automatic data import and fast script evaluation; Unreal importer with full Blueprint support and an automatic dialogue traversal engine implemented as a Flow Player actor component) | Differentiator — a defining feature that separates "narrative game middleware" (Ink, Articy) from pure IF/hyperfiction tools (Twine, ChoiceScript) |
| **Direct web/HTML publishing without a game engine** | Compiles the finished branching story straight to a standalone webpage, playable anywhere, with no engine or install required | [Twine](https://twinery.org/) ("publishes directly to HTML... work can be posted nearly anywhere," output is free to use commercially), [Ink/Inky](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/) ("export to the web, generating a page ready to be customised") | Table stakes for browser-first IF tools (Twine, Ink); not applicable to Articy's engine-middleware model |
| **Swappable story formats/engines within one authoring tool** | Lets the same visual editor produce output in different underlying scripting dialects, each with its own macro syntax and capability set, without switching applications | [Twine](https://twinery.org/) (built-in story formats Chapbook, Harlowe, Snowman, and SugarCube — [each with distinct macro syntax](https://twinery.org/cookbook/terms/terms_macros.html): Harlowe uses parentheses/brackets e.g. `(font: "Arial")[...]`, SugarCube and Twine 1 use `<<macro>>`, Snowman embeds raw JavaScript with `<% %>`, Chapbook uses inserts/modifiers) | Differentiator — unique to Twine among the reviewed tools |
| **Dialogue-fragment batch authoring** | Speeds up writing branching dialogue by letting an author add several connected dialogue fragments at once, with speaker and template pre-assigned, and see suggested next-fragment combinations based on existing flow content | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Quick Create: adds multiple dialogue fragments at once with speaker/template pre-assigned, displays useful combinations as suggestions "especially useful when branching out") | Differentiator |
| **Screenplay/document-to-flow conversion** | Converts screenplay-formatted or script-like text into the tool's visual node/flow representation by drag-and-drop, bridging prose-first and graph-first authoring | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Document-to-Flow conversion; Document View supports writing dialogue in script-like format that converts into the Flow editor) | Differentiator |
| **Voice-over pipeline management** | Manages placeholder and final audio files for spoken lines, including auto-generated text-to-speech stand-ins that can be reviewed in-context before final recording | [Articy:Draft](https://www.articy.com/en/features/) (Voice-over Management plugin: creates empty WAV files or TTS placeholders, reviewable in Simulation mode, exports spoken lines/filenames to Excel) | Differentiator — clearly aimed at professional game-VO pipelines, not literary IF |
| **Beginner-friendly plain-text scripting aimed at non-programmers** | A scripting language deliberately designed so an author with no programming background can write a fully branching, stat-driven interactive novel | [ChoiceScript](https://www.choiceofgames.com/make-your-own-games/choicescript-intro/) (explicitly positioned as "easy and fun, even for authors with no programming experience"), [Ink](https://www.inklestudios.com/ink/) ("markup, not programming: text comes first, with code and logic inserted within... simple, elegant syntax... easy to learn") | Table stakes for the author-oriented (non-engine-middleware) IF tools; contrasts with Articy's more technical, game-team-oriented tooling |

## Notes on source coverage and gaps

- **yWriter5**'s own vendor page ([spacejock.com](https://www.spacejock.com/yWriter5.html)) is extremely sparse; most concrete feature detail above comes instead from the [official Quickstart Guide PDF](http://www.spacejock.com/files/yWriter5Guide.pdf) and a third-party [PCMag review](https://www.pcmag.com/reviews/ywriter).
- **World Anvil**'s page focused specifically on the worldbuilding wiki did not surface relationship-map, timeline, or export/compile features — these may exist elsewhere in the product but are not evidenced by the fetched page, so they are marked absent rather than assumed.
- **Obsidian** itself is a general note-taking app, not a purpose-built writing tool; its writer-relevant capability comes almost entirely from community plugins ([Longform](https://github.com/kevboh/longform/blob/main/README.md) for manuscript/scene management and [World Builder](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/world-builder) for entity tracking), layered on top of Obsidian's core linking, graph, and Canvas features ([obsidian.md](https://obsidian.md/)).
- **Twine**'s official homepage is minimal by design (it emphasizes philosophy — free, open, ownership of output — over a feature list); deeper mechanics (story format differences, macro syntax) were sourced from the [Twine Cookbook](https://twinery.org/cookbook/terms/terms_macros.html).
- **ChoiceScript** and **Ink** are scripting languages/toolchains rather than GUI apps in the traditional sense; their "authoring surface" is closer to a code editor (Inky for Ink) or a plain-text file plus local server (ChoiceScript).
- **Vellum** and **ProWritingAid** were added as supplementary sources (beyond the 14 required apps) specifically because they represent the state of the art for the Export/Publishing and Revision/Review categories respectively, and several of the 14 required apps (e.g., Atticus) explicitly benchmark themselves against Vellum.
