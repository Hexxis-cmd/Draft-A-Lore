## How to get a license

Open an issue or start a discussion at
<https://github.com/Hexxis-cmd/Draft-A-Lore>, or contact **Daymien Vanhorn** through the
GitHub profile at <https://github.com/Hexxis-cmd>.

Include:

1. Who you are and the company name.
2. What you want to do with Draft A Lore, and how it reaches end users.
3. Expected scale — seats, users, or units.
4. Whether you need to modify it, rebrand it, or keep your changes private.

| **Rebranding / white-label** | Shipping it under your own name | Higher fee; explicitly lifts the reserved-name and reserved-artwork terms in writing |
| **OEM / redistribution** | Preinstalling or redistributing at scale | Per-unit or tiered volume pricing |

Any commercial license will normally cover: the scope of permitted use, the term, whether
the credit requirement is kept or waived, whether the "Draft A Lore" name and logo may be
